Extra sign-in domains

Authelia can protect only sites under its sign-in domains: a browser sends its session cookie to one registrable domain. Add a domain here to protect sites under it too. Each sign-in domain has a portal of its own, where its sites send visitors to sign in.

Users, passwords and second factors are the same on every domain, but a sign-in on one domain does not sign the user in on another.
