Your rules

Rules added here are kept separately from the list that ships with the software, and are applied on top of it. That matters both ways: a deploy replaces the shipped list and cannot take yours with it, and a shipped signature you switch off stays off when the list is next updated. A signature that caused a false ban must not come back with a release.

A signature that begins with a slash matches the whole path. Anything else matches one path segment wherever it appears, so a signature of acme-console also catches /x/acme-console/login.

Shipped rules

What the engine looks for in a request. A decisive category is one no client has any business asking for, so a single hit is enough on its own. The rest are only probable: a site may genuinely run WordPress or serve something under /vendor, so one hit there is not enough by itself.

Switch off any signature that is matching your own traffic. It stays off across updates, and you can switch it back on here.

The shipped list is a file on the gateway, replaceable without rebuilding anything. A broken or missing one keeps the built-in rules rather than switching detection off.
